# Paper

## Purpose

This folder will hold the IEEE-style paper skeleton and final research artifacts.

## Why It Exists

The implementation should produce explainable results that can be written into a formal academic report. Keeping paper material in its own folder helps connect experiments to claims, tables, figures, and limitations.

## Planned Contents

- IEEE paper outline
- Related work notes
- Methods descriptions
- Experiment tables
- Figures
- Limitations and future work
